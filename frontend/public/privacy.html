Who we are

India In-Time ("we", "us") is a travel-planning app. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Registered entity (if any): [ENTITY NAME / ADDRESS].

What we collect

  • Account info: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo via Firebase Authentication.
  • Trip data: itineraries, saved plans, budget entries, and "passport" stamps you create in the app, stored in our database so they sync across your devices.
  • Location: if you grant location permission, your device GPS coordinates are used to center the map, suggest nearby places, and power live-navigation features. This is used in-session and is not sold or shared with advertisers.
  • AI-processed content: trip preferences, photos you submit to AI tools (photo captions, food-safety scanner, sign/menu translation, AI Lens), and chat messages you send to the in-app assistant are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses.
  • Feedback: star ratings, category tags, and free-text comments you submit are stored to help us improve the app.
  • Usage analytics: we use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate usage patterns.
  • Device/technical data: standard web request metadata (IP address, browser type) collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure for security and rate-limiting.

How we use it

  • To create, save, and sync your itineraries across devices.
  • To generate AI-powered recommendations, captions, translations, and trip advice.
  • To operate core features: routing, weather, crowd predictions, and safety tools.
  • To maintain security (rate limiting, abuse prevention) and to improve the app based on aggregate usage and feedback.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties we share data with

  • Google (Firebase): authentication.
  • Google (Gemini API): processes content you submit to AI features.
  • Google Analytics: aggregate usage analytics.
