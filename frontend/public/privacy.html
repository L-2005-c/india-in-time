Privacy & Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026 · Founder: Lokesh Chilukuri

At India In-Time ("we", "us", "our"), your digital privacy and data security are foundational principles. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your information when you use our web application and travel intelligence services, in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable data protection regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Identity: When you sign in using Google Authentication via Firebase, we receive your name, email address, unique Firebase user ID, and profile picture avatar.
  • Itinerary & Travel Data: Custom itineraries, saved trip plans, visited spots, expense split records, and digital passport stamps generated in the app are stored in our secure database to enable seamless multi-device synchronization.
  • Real-Time Geolocation (Optional): If you grant browser GPS permissions, your coordinates are processed strictly client-side to center the interactive map, compute realistic route distances, and provide nearby landmark recommendations. We do not store historical GPS tracking logs on our servers.
  • AI Assistant & Multimodal Content: Prompts, travel preferences, and uploaded media (such as landmark photos or signage images submitted to the AI Lens or Translation tools) are securely transmitted to Google's Gemini API for instantaneous inference.
  • Usage & Telemetry Data: We utilize Google Analytics (GA4) with IP anonymization to gather high-level aggregate traffic metrics (e.g., popular city selections, feature usage) to enhance application performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your information strictly for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To generate, optimize, and synchronize your custom travel plans across your devices.
  • To power real-time intelligence engines: road routing, weather pivots, crowd predictions, and cultural schedules.
  • To safeguard system reliability through automated rate-limiting, bot prevention, and abuse detection.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or travel history to third-party advertisers or brokers.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Processors

To deliver high-availability, low-latency services, India In-Time partners with industry-leading infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Secure OAuth authentication and session token verification.
  • Google Gemini API: Generative AI text and vision models.
  • PostgreSQL Database (Neon / Cloud SQL): Encrypted persistent storage for user itineraries and cached place metadata.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Privacy-preserving aggregated analytics.
  • Open-Meteo & OSRM: Public weather datasets and open road routing algorithms.

4. Data Retention & User Control

🛡️ Complete User Control:
You can clear all device-stored cached plans, offline map tiles, and session tokens at any time directly in the app by visiting Settings → Clear Device Data.

Your saved cloud itineraries remain associated with your account until you delete them or request full account erasure.

5. Your Rights Under Indian Law (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a user of India In-Time, you have the right to:

  • Right of Access: Request a summary of personal data and processing activities associated with your account.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request the correction of inaccurate data or complete deletion of your account and stored trips.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Contact our designated Data Grievance Officer for prompt resolution of privacy concerns.

6. Security Measures

We enforce strict defense-in-depth security standards: SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, strict Content Security Policy (CSP) headers, isolated rate limiters, and secure HTTP-only session cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

India In-Time is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements or regulatory requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
